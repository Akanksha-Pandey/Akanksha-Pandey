# Akanksha Pandey

Computational biologist with a background in oncology drug discovery and evolutionary genomics. I build bioinformatics pipelines, apply machine learning to biological data, and care a lot about reproducible, well-documented science.

## What I work on

**Cancer genomics & drug discovery** -- At Kronos Bio (2020-2023), I worked on target identification, biomarker discovery, and drug-combination strategies across hematological malignancies, TNBC, SCLC, and HPV-driven cancers. This included ML frameworks for mechanism-of-action studies, drug synergy quantification from PRISM screens across ~950 cell lines, custom RNA-seq pipelines, and a large-scale collaboration with the Broad Institute screening >100 compounds. Six conference posters came out of this work.

**Phylogenomics (PhD)** -- My PhD at the University of Florida focused on why standard evolutionary models get deep phylogenies wrong. I developed structure-aware substitution models that account for how protein sites in different structural environments (surface vs. core) evolve under different constraints, and trained clade-specific models across birds, mammals, plants, and fungi. Three first-author papers and contributions to the Avian Phylogenomics Consortium.

**Statistical genetics & proteomics** -- Recent independent work includes a case-control GWAS for early-onset pancreatic cancer (see [eopc-gwas](https://github.com/Akanksha-Pandey/eopc-gwas)) and high-dimensional proteomics analysis from the Ornish Alzheimer's trial (see [ornish-proteomics](https://github.com/Akanksha-Pandey/ornish-proteomics)).

**Pipeline development** -- Nextflow/DSL2 workflows for NGS data, containerized with Docker, deployable on AWS and HPC.
